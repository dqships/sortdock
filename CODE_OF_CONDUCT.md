# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want SortDock to be a welcoming project. Be respectful, kind, and direct.
Disagreement is fine. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats,
and sharing someone else's private information are not.

## Expected behavior

- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask questions when something is unclear.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and points of view.
- Help keep discussion useful and on topic.

## Reporting a concern

For conduct concerns, contact the project maintainer privately through the
[dqships GitHub profile](https://github.com/dqships). Please
include enough detail to help us understand what happened. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when this code of conduct is not followed.
