# Keep Downloads organized

SortDock watches Downloads or another folder you choose, then routes new files with simple rules you control.

- Route files by words in their names, such as invoice or receipt.
- Use file-type rules when no keyword matches.
- Choose whether SortDock asks in the menu bar or moves matching files automatically.
- Pause sorting any time.
- Keep your rules and activity on your Mac.
- Give SortDock access only to folders you select.

SortDock never overwrites an existing file. When a name is already in use, it safely saves the new file with a name like `example (1).pdf`.

No account. No cloud upload. Your files stay on your Mac.
