# App Store Connect checklist

- Confirm existing macOS record before creating one.
- Confirm `com.followusai.SortDock` and build number availability.
- Use English (U.S.), Productivity, Apple standard EULA, and truthful age/content/privacy answers.
- Upload final screenshots and save each field, then reload to verify persistence.
- Do not choose pricing or availability without an established product decision.
- Do not add or submit for review.
